## Summary

src/state/sequencer.rs is an unusually disciplined large module for its risk class: the apply path is Result-threaded end-to-end with four provenance-preserving error enums, the K1 dual-counter and commit-ordering invariants are written down at both declaration and mutation sites and locked by exact-value tests (including a hand-fabricated corrupt-CAS fail-closed test that asserts error class, zero L4.E continuation, and zero logical_t consumption), live monetary invariants are asserted in the dispatch arms rather than only in fixtures, and a 9592-line scan finds zero TODO/FIXME/allow(dead_code) and essentially one dead enum variant. The port-readiness frictions are concentrated at the boundary, not in the logic: the constructor's 10-parameter fan of internal subsystem types makes facade-wrapping expensive; the pub surface carries a panicking wrapper, a debug-build whole-state injection helper, and ~24 admission-root helpers; the module's own top-of-file doc still describes a long-retired stub state; lock-poison handling is inconsistent (hard error on the rejection path, silent classification degradation in the refinement path, one misnamed poison variant covering four different locks); and the agent/system ingress counters share the L4.E submit_id keyspace. None of these are correctness defects under the current single-writer deployment; all eight are recorded as advisory RiskFindings for the porting decision.
