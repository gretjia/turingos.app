## Correct Reading

```text
homogeneous agents
abundant budget
Lean proof-tree search
price used as a parent-routing signal
```

Within that regime, real price routing did not beat its controls in the
reported snapshot. The result is useful and directionally important, but it
must not be inflated into a broad market-economy verdict.
